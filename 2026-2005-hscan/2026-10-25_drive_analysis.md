## 1. Alignment: Hermes's offset is 91.8 s early
- **Hermes's offset is wrong.** `--ref-offset 1792955689.792`, the first 7E1 poll at 12:14:49.79, gives junk: every DID fits its own FORScan column at R² < 0.76.
- FORScan's CSV evidently starts when recording started, not at the first poll. DID polling on the bus starts 91.8 s before CSV t=0, and the DID replies run until CSV t≈1359.
- **Fix:** cross-correlating CSV RPM against PCM DID 0x1165/4 has one sharp peak at a shift of 91.78 s (R² 0.999996, 1737 samples). **Use `--ref-offset 1792955781.572`.** With it:
  - Every DID matches its FORScan column at lag 0.00 s (table below).
  - The speed edges line up (plot panel 2).
- **Independent check against the passenger's wall-clock calls:**
  - The tow/haul flag (0x225 b0 = 0x60) is on 338.4–588.3 s. Hermes's tow/haul on/off marks fall at 341.3 / 594.4 s.
  - 0x230 goes to Park at 1207.3 s, after a brief park at 1180–1188 s. The "parked" call is at 1221.7 s.
  - Both agree within the few-second call latency.
- **Log end vs key-off:** the CAN log ends at 1501.6 s (12:41:23.2) with the engine still idling at ~690 rpm. Hermes's key-off mark is later (12:41:47) and his notes say the log ran 30 s past it, but there's no key-off in the file.

## 4. Caveats
- **The ground jumper was never confirmed.** The adapter TX count is 0, and the log is clean: no stray IDs, steady periods, 0 decode errors against the DBC.
- **Weak references:**
  - TFT: 3 broadcast values, 71–74 °C.
  - GEAR: codes 1/2/3/5/6 with few samples outside 6. Code 2 has only 5 polls, code 4 none.
  - EOT: 79–89 °C.
  - APP: never above 31 %.
  - LINEDSD: mostly one value.
- No ECT, baro or brake PID was logged, so the hot ECT end, the boost zero and the brake candidate are unverified.
- Correlation can't separate pedal, TCC and decel states. They change together on a normal drive.
